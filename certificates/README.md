# How to Add Your Certificates

## Step 1: Prepare Your Certificate Files

For each certificate, you need TWO files:
1. **Image file** (JPG or PNG) - A screenshot or image of your certificate
2. **PDF file** - The original PDF certificate (optional, but recommended)

## Step 2: Save Files to the Certificates Folder

Save your certificate files in the `certificates` folder with these names:

### For Google Digital Marketing Certificate:
- `google-digital-marketing.jpg` - Image preview
- `google-digital-marketing.pdf` - Full PDF (optional)

### For Other Certificates:
- `certificate2.jpg` and `certificate2.pdf`
- `certificate3.jpg` and `certificate3.pdf`

## Step 3: Convert PDF to Image (if needed)

If you only have PDF files, you can:
1. Open the PDF
2. Take a screenshot
3. Save as JPG or PNG
4. Place in the certificates folder

## Step 4: Update Certificate Information

Edit `index.html` and update the certificate details:
- Certificate name
- Issuing organization
- Year

## Example Structure:

```
portfolio/
├── certificates/
│   ├── google-digital-marketing.jpg
│   ├── google-digital-marketing.pdf
│   ├── certificate2.jpg
│   ├── certificate2.pdf
│   ├── certificate3.jpg
│   └── certificate3.pdf
├── index.html
├── style.css

```

## Features:

✅ Certificate images display in cards
✅ Hover over certificate to see "View Certificate" button
✅ Click to open full PDF in new tab
